# Polymarket five-league football (soccer) research

This was a research-only track: **no football live trades or realized trading P&L**. The initial October 30–November 1 study considered 404 finished matches and 377 gated cases. A separate November 4 study examined 2,204 match records (2,200 analysis-ready) and about 1,400 rule variants. Keep the cohorts distinct. Apparent profit from minute-price history and a goal-following VAR survivor bias did not survive executable-print and data-quality checks.

See docs/research-chronology.md, reports/ and results/ for hypotheses, corrected tests and QA. scripts/ contains selected research scripts. data/public-match-index.csv has 2,204 public match-metadata rows; data/football-book-sample-coverage.csv records coverage for 48 weekend matches and 17,263 order-book snapshots. The raw books and bulk third-party/API tables are excluded.

The work was paused, not deployed. Full reproduction needs original data/API access. See ../docs/DATA_POLICY.md.
