# soccer_data: data dictionary

Big-5 European league matches listed on Polymarket: 2025-26 (Aug 2025 to May 2026) and 2026-27 through 2026-11-03 (kickoff, UTC).
Built 2026-11-04 by workstream `soccer_data`. All tables are in `data/soccer_data/` (paths below are relative to
`polymarket/research/auto-20261104/`). Generated by `scripts/soccer_data/10_data_dictionary.py` from the parquet schemas.
Coverage, counts and every known problem are in `results/soccer_data/REPORT.md`.

## Tables and grain

| table | grain | primary key | main foreign keys |
|---|---|---|---|
| `matches` | one Polymarket league-match event (1X2 event) | `match_id` | `espn_event_id` -> ESPN; odds via `odds.match_id` or `odds.espn_event_id` |
| `markets` | one market: three 1X2 (`home`, `draw`, `away`) per match, plus `ou25` and `btts` when listed | `condition_id` | `match_id` |
| `price_history` | one CLOB `prices-history` point (about 1 per minute) of token0 of a market | (`token_id`, `ts`) | `match_id`, `condition_id` |
| `trades` | one taker fill (Data API v2 `/v2/trades`, `taker_only=true`) of a 1X2 market, YES or NO token | (`condition_id`, `seq_in_market`) | `match_id`, `condition_id`, `token_id` |
| `events` | one ESPN key event (goals, cards, kickoff, half-time, full-time, subs, delays) or ESPN commentary VAR decision | (`match_id`, `seq`) | `match_id`, `espn_event_id` |
| `odds` | one football-data.co.uk match row | (`league`, `season`, `date`, `fd_home`, `fd_away`) | `match_id` (primary Polymarket event), `espn_event_id` |

Recommended filter: `matches.analysis_ready` (primary event, Polymarket resolution equals the ESPN result, ESPN status
full time). It drops the postponed and unresolved match, the abandoned match and the secondary event of each duplicate pair.

## Conventions

- Time: every `*_ts` column is integer epoch seconds UTC. Every `*_utc` column is a timezone-aware UTC timestamp. The only
  local time is `odds.fd_time_uk` (UK local time, Europe/London). Trade prints have one-second resolution.
- `matches.kickoff_ts` is the canonical scheduled kickoff: the ESPN scheduled time when the match is linked to ESPN (tracks
  postponements), otherwise Polymarket `gameStartTime`. Polymarket `gameStartTime` (`pm_game_start_ts`) is stale for
  23 re-dated matches (16 by more than 30 minutes, up to 66 days); never use it for alignment.
  `matches.kickoff_wall_ts` is the actual kickoff from the ESPN wallclock.
- Prices are USDC per share (0 to 1, a probability scale). `size` is in shares (outcome tokens). Notional is in USDC.
  Odds are decimal odds.
- 1X2 roles come from the Polymarket slug: `home` = first-listed team (`home_code`), `away` = second. Each 1X2 role is a
  separate binary market (YES pays 1). `token0` = YES (or Over for `ou25`), `token1` = NO (Under).
  Exception: `fl1-psg-ren-2026-08-23` is listed by Polymarket as PSG vs Rennes, but Rennes was at home (ESPN and
  football-data agree). `matches.home_away_swapped` and `odds.pm_orientation_swapped` flag it. Scores in `matches` and
  `events.team_side_pm` are expressed in Polymarket orientation; `events.team_side` and `espn_*` columns are in ESPN orientation.
- Trades contain fills on both tokens. Use `yes_price` (YES scale) and `taker_buys_yes` to put them on one axis.
  `side` is the taker's side for `token_id`. Fees are not deducted anywhere; see `markets.fee_rate` (taker fee =
  shares x rate x p x (1 - p)).
- Team names: canonical names are ESPN `displayName`s. Maps: `data/soccer_data/team_map_pm_espn.csv` (Polymarket title
  names, which change spelling between seasons, to ESPN) and `data/soccer_data/team_map_fd_espn.csv` (football-data names to ESPN).

## Join recipes

```python
import pandas as pd
D = 'data/soccer_data/'
m  = pd.read_parquet(D + 'matches.parquet').query('analysis_ready')
mk = pd.read_parquet(D + 'markets.parquet')
x12 = mk[mk.role.isin(['home', 'draw', 'away'])]                    # 3 rows per match
tr = pd.read_parquet(D + 'trades.parquet', filters=[('season', '=', '2026-27')])
ev = pd.read_parquet(D + 'events.parquet').query('scoring_play')      # goals; wallclock_ts is second-level
od = pd.read_parquet(D + 'odds.parquet')
m_odds = m.merge(od, on='espn_event_id', how='left', suffixes=('', '_fd'))   # reaches both events of a duplicate pair
goal_market = ev.merge(x12, left_on=['match_id', 'team_side_pm'], right_on=['match_id', 'role'])  # scorer's win market
```

## Known gaps (details and counts in REPORT.md)

- Polymarket did not list 3 of the 1,752 big-5 fixtures in 2025-26 (Atalanta-Pisa 2025-08-24, PSG-Strasbourg 2025-10-17,
  Nice-Lyon 2025-10-18). They are absent from every table except `odds` (where `match_id` is null).
- Over/Under 2.5 and BTTS markets exist only from October 2025 (sibling `-more-markets` event); not every match has them.
- `trades` is limited to the three 1X2 markets; O/U 2.5 and BTTS have metadata and minute prices only.
- `price_history` is a minute snapshot from CLOB `prices-history`, window [kickoff - 6 h, kickoff + 2.5 h]. It lags
  real prints around goals; use `trades` for anything event-driven. Points stop when a market closes.
- Polymarket in-play trading halts with no prints at all (not missing data: prints sum exactly to Gamma volume) are
  listed in `results/soccer_data/qa_trading_gaps.csv`.
- ESPN wallclocks are second-level and align with the market reaction to goals to within seconds for most goals (see
  REPORT, "Event timestamp precision"). VAR decisions come from ESPN commentary (`events.source == 'commentary'`); a few lack a wallclock.
- Pinnacle odds (`PS*`, `P*`) exist for 2025-26 only; 2026-27 football-data files have Bet365, Betfair exchange,
  market maximum and average only.
